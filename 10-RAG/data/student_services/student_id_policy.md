# Student Identification Card Policy

## Student ID

Every enrolled student is issued a university student identification card. The card is used for identification and may also be required for library services, examinations, laboratories, and other university facilities.

## Lost ID

A student who loses an ID card should report the loss to the student administration office as soon as possible.

A replacement card may require payment of a replacement fee.

## Use of ID

Students must not lend their student ID to another person. Misuse of an ID may be handled under university disciplinary procedures.
