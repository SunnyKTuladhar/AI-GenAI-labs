# Student Leave and Temporary Absence Policy

## Short-Term Leave

Students who need to be absent from classes for personal, medical, or other legitimate reasons should notify the relevant course instructor or department as early as possible.

Approval of leave does not automatically remove the student's responsibility to meet attendance requirements.

## Extended Absence

Students expecting to be absent for an extended period should contact their academic department and student services office to discuss available options.

Students should not assume that an extended absence automatically results in a semester break or withdrawal.

## Semester Break

A student who needs to take an approved semester break must submit the required request through student services and receive formal approval.

The conditions and maximum duration of an approved break depend on the student's program and applicable academic regulations.

## Returning After Leave

Students returning from an approved extended leave may be required to complete reactivation or registration procedures before attending classes.
