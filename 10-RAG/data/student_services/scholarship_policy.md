# Student Scholarship Policy

## Eligibility

University scholarships may be available to students based on academic performance, financial need, leadership, community involvement, or other criteria announced by the university.

Eligibility criteria vary by scholarship.

## Application

Students must submit scholarship applications by the published deadline and provide the documents requested for the specific scholarship.

Late applications may not be considered.

## Academic Requirement

Some merit-based scholarships require students to maintain a minimum semester or cumulative GPA. The required GPA is specified in the individual scholarship announcement.

## Continuation

Receiving a scholarship in one semester does not automatically guarantee the scholarship in future semesters. Students must continue to meet the conditions of the relevant scholarship program.
