# Grading and Academic Performance Policy

## Grading Scale

Everest International University uses the following undergraduate grading scale:

- A: 90–100
- B+: 85–89
- B: 80–84
- C+: 75–79
- C: 70–74
- D: 60–69
- F: below 60

## Passing a Course

A student must receive at least a D grade to pass an undergraduate course. Some programs may impose higher minimum grades for prerequisite or major courses.

## Grade Point Average

Semester GPA is calculated using the grade points earned in courses completed during that semester, weighted by course credits.

Cumulative GPA includes all applicable completed courses according to university academic regulations.

## Grade Review

A student who believes a final grade contains an administrative or calculation error may submit a grade review request to the department within seven calendar days after grades are published.
