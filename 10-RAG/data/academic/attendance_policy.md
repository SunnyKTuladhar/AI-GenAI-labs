# Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students enrolled at Everest International University (EIU) must maintain at least 80% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to sit for the final examination.

Attendance is calculated separately for each course. A student's overall semester attendance does not replace the attendance requirement for an individual course.

## Attendance Shortage

Students whose attendance falls below 80% may be prevented from taking the final examination unless they receive an approved attendance waiver.

Requests for an attendance waiver must be submitted to the department office with supporting documentation before the examination period begins.

## Medical and Emergency Absence

Documented medical emergencies and serious family emergencies may be considered when reviewing an attendance waiver. Approval is not automatic and is subject to departmental review.

## Attendance Records

Students should check their attendance records regularly through the student portal. Any discrepancy should be reported to the course instructor within seven days of the attendance record being posted.
