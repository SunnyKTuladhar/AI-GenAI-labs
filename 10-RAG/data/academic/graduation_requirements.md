# Undergraduate Graduation Requirements

## Credit Requirement

An undergraduate student must successfully complete at least 120 academic credits to graduate, unless the student's approved degree program specifies a different requirement.

## Academic Standing

Students must satisfy all required courses, prerequisite requirements, internship or project requirements, and other conditions listed in their degree program.

## Final Project

Programs that include a final-year project require students to receive a passing grade for the project before graduation clearance can be granted.

## Graduation Clearance

Students apply for graduation clearance through the academic administration office. The university checks completed credits, required courses, outstanding fees, library obligations, and other administrative requirements before approving clearance.
