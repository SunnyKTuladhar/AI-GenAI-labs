# University Library Rules

## Opening Hours

The EIU Central Library is open:

- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

Opening hours may be changed during public holidays and university-wide closures.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students may borrow up to five books at one time.

Books are normally issued for 14 days. A renewal may be requested before the due date if no other user has reserved the item.

## Overdue Items

Students are responsible for returning borrowed materials by the due date. Borrowing privileges may be temporarily suspended when overdue items remain unresolved.

## Library Card

Students use their university student ID as their library identification. Students must report a lost ID to the relevant university office.

## Study Areas

Quiet study areas require students to keep conversations and device volume low. Food and drinks are not permitted in designated silent study areas.
