# Makeup and Deferred Examination Policy

## Eligibility

A student who misses a final examination because of a documented medical emergency, serious family emergency, or another circumstance accepted by the university may request a deferred or makeup examination.

Missing an examination without an accepted reason does not automatically qualify a student for a makeup examination.

## Application Deadline

A makeup examination request should normally be submitted to the department within five working days of the missed examination.

The student must provide supporting documentation when requested.

## Approval

The department reviews the request and supporting evidence. Submission of a request does not guarantee approval.

## Makeup Examination

Approved students receive information about the makeup examination date, location, and permitted materials from the department.

A student should not assume that the makeup examination will have the same questions or format as the original examination.
