# Examination Rules

## Examination Eligibility

Students must satisfy the attendance requirement and complete required academic and administrative obligations before taking final examinations.

A student who is not eligible for an examination must contact the department office before the examination date.

## Examination Identification

Students must bring their valid university student ID card to an examination. The examination supervisor may require additional identification when necessary.

## Examination Conduct

Students must arrive at the examination room at least 15 minutes before the scheduled starting time.

Electronic devices, including mobile phones and smart watches, must be switched off and stored as directed by examination supervisors unless the examination instructions explicitly permit them.

Students must follow the instructions of examination supervisors throughout the examination.

## Late Arrival

Students arriving more than 30 minutes after the scheduled start time may be refused entry unless the chief examination officer grants an exception.

## Academic Integrity

Unauthorized assistance, copying, impersonation, use of prohibited materials, or other forms of examination misconduct may result in disciplinary action under the university's academic integrity procedures.
