# Undergraduate Admission Requirements

## General Requirement

Applicants for undergraduate programs must have completed the educational qualification required by the selected degree program.

Applicants must provide academic transcripts or certificates, a valid government-issued identification document, a recent photograph, and any additional documents specified by the program.

## Application Process

The standard application process is:

1. Complete the online application form.
2. Upload the required documents.
3. Pay the application fee, if applicable.
4. Wait for document verification.
5. Complete any required entrance assessment or interview.
6. Receive the admission decision.

## Document Verification

The admissions office may request original documents or additional evidence for verification.

Providing false or misleading information can result in cancellation of an application or admission.

## International Applicants

International applicants may have additional requirements related to immigration, language proficiency, educational equivalency, and financial documentation.
